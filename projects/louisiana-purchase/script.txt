CLEAN COPY-READY SCRIPT
America sent two men to buy one city. They came home with half a continent. Price? Fifteen million dollars — about three cents an acre. This is how the United States doubled overnight, by accident. Rewind. 1802. France and Britain have just made peace, but nobody trusts it. Both smile, shake hands, and hide daggers behind their backs. War is coming back. Everyone knows it. Napoleon has a dream: rebuild France's empire in America. His key piece is Louisiana, a vast territory stretching from New Orleans all the way to Canada. On paper, it makes France a world power again. But the engine of that empire is not Louisiana. It's Saint-Domingue, today's Haiti — France's richest colony, pumping out sugar and coffee. A money machine, built on slavery. Then the enslaved people rise up. Chains break. Plantations burn. The colony Napoleon needs most slips out of his hands, battle by battle. He sends a huge army to take it back. At first, it works. Then yellow fever hits. Soldiers collapse. France loses the island for good — and Haiti will become independent. Without Saint-Domingue, Louisiana suddenly has no purpose. Meanwhile, America's western farmers depend on one choke point: the Mississippi River, and the port of New Orleans. Block that port, and their trade chokes. So President Thomas Jefferson sends two diplomats, Monroe and Livingston, to Paris with a simple mission: buy New Orleans, and only New Orleans, for up to ten million dollars. But Napoleon is doing different math. War with Britain is coming. He needs cash, fast. Louisiana is far away, hard to defend, and Britain could simply take it. His wallet is empty. So he shocks everyone. Not just the city. All of Louisiana. Every river, every plain, eight hundred thousand square miles — for fifteen million dollars. The diplomats have no permission for this. None. But some deals are too big to phone home about. On April 30, 1803, they sign anyway. Overnight, America doubles in size, for roughly four cents an acre. Two men went shopping for a city — and accidentally bought an empire.
