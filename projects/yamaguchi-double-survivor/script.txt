August 6th, 1945. Tsutomu Yamaguchi, a 29-year-old engineer, is finishing a business trip in Hiroshima, when the sky explodes. A single bomb flattens the city. Burned, half-blind, he survives, and does the only thing he can think of. He goes home. Three days later, in Nagasaki, he's telling his boss what he saw in Hiroshima. His boss doesn't believe him. No weapon, he says, could do that to a city. And then, the sky turns white again. A second bomb. Same man. Same nightmare. Twice. Yamaguchi lived to 93, and became the only person in history officially recognised as surviving both atomic bombs. One man. Two bombs. And a story the world must never forget.
